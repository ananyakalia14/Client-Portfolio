Bringing and to a very B2B booth

Plane X FOSS'25

  • Creative Direction
  • Design

Plane was a platinum sponsor at IndiaFOSS 25, the 5th edition of India's biggest open source conference, run by the FOSS United community. two days in Bangalore, 20–21 Sept 2025.

we had a booth, so naturally i made myself in charge of making everything pretty. that meant the t-shirts, the standees, the box, and the star of the show, a life-size canvas that people could actually walk up to. but if you ask me, my favourite of the lot were the stickers.

i had an idea to gamify the canvas and bring my inner koffee-with-karan queen out. i really wanted this canvas to stand out, so i very intentionally didn't follow our brand colors, and you won't find our logo anywhere on it. instead, the board got colors that were playful and fun, the kind that make you want to walk over. and the whole thing was analog. no screens. the kids wrote out their answers by hand, and then got to see them up on display for everyone to read. their answers, out in the open. all in the spirit of open-source.

POV: you're the photographer, videographer, social media team, coffee-maker, all at once :P

the most most fun and satisfying mini project i've worked on!

IMPACT

and it worked. we were hands down the most visited booth in the hall, and there were 40 of us. the board filled up so fast that by the end of day one, we had to pull some stickers off just to make room for more. the canvas did exactly what i hoped it would. people walked over, stayed, and got talking to us about open source, and about Plane. the stickers and the t-shirts sold out like hot cakes. and yes, we were the most talked about booth that year. not that i'm keeping count. 😉
