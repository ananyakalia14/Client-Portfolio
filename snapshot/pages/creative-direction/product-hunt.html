my first full-blown, last-minute tornado campaign: Product Hunt.

Plane X PH.

  • Creative Direction
  • Campaigns
  • Video
  • Ads
  • Social Media

i had less than a week.

that's how Plane's first Product Hunt launch started. there was no plan, just a deadline, one junior designer, and me. i had never run a Product Hunt launch in my life. if you're wondering whether that's as scary as it sounds, it is.

here's what was on my plate.

launch video

first, a launch video. here's the thing about launch videos: nobody gives you a minute. you get ten seconds. maybe. so those ten seconds had one job. show you how powerful Plane is before your thumb moves on. not a feature list, not a logo reveal, just a glimpse. if you left at second eleven, you should still leave knowing Plane could run your team's work.

the other fifty seconds are for the people who stay, and if you stay, you deserve something for it. Plane is a big, complicated product. big products love to say everything at once. this one couldn't. it had to be simple. it had to be straightforward. and it had to be cool enough to make you stop scrolling.

storyboard

teaser pop-up

then, a pop-up. it's my favourite thing i made for the launch, and the one i had to fight hardest to keep on the website. three days out, i wanted something living on Plane's homepage that told people a launch was coming without selling them anything. everyone hates pop-ups, so this one leaned into it. on paper it sounded like a bad idea: an extra box on the homepage, up early, with nothing to sell. but it made people smile, and it made them remember the date, and three days before a launch that's the whole job. so i fought for it, and it stayed.

ads

the run-up

then came the noise. there were ads and social media. there were the Product Hunt communities, where you can't just show up and sell. you have to show up and talk. and there were our customers, who needed to know a big day was coming, and a reason to care when it did.

socials

d-day

and then, d-day. every asset the page needed. the maker comment, which is the first thing you read from a maker, so it has to sound like a person and not a press release. plus a long list of small things nobody notices until they're missing.

  • maker comment

what i learnt

i, obviously, made a million mistakes. i learnt Product Hunt the way you learn to swim when someone throws you in. drown, try to float, drown again, pray for life, and the first stroke. the first taste of victory.

beyond everything, i learnt what accountability means. not the word you throw around to get hired. the real dirty thing. when it's your launch, there's no one to hand it to. if something breaks, it's yours. if something works, you don't stop to celebrate.

i walked in with zero idea how Product Hunt runs. i walked out able to run one end to end. even today, literally today, i get messages from the Product Hunt community to support. impact. :)
